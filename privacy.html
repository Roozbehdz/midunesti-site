Privacy policy

midunesti uploader · last updated 20 November 2026

What this app is

“midunesti uploader” is a private tool used by the owner of the می‌دونستی؟ (midunesti) channels to upload and schedule their own videos on YouTube and Instagram and to read their own channel statistics. It is not offered to the public and no one else signs in to it.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner’s channel (youtube.upload), to read the owner’s channel and video details (youtube.readonly), and to read the owner’s YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly).
  • Instagram: permission to publish to and read insights for the owner’s own professional account.

How it is used and stored

Access tokens and statistics (views, likes, saves, shares) are stored only on the owner’s own computer and are used only to publish the owner’s videos and to decide which topics work best. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Videos are briefly placed in the owner’s cloud storage so Instagram can fetch them, and are deleted after publishing.

Viewers

The app does not collect any data about people who watch the videos. Viewer data is handled by YouTube and Instagram under their own policies.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
